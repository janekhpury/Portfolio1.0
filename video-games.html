Game 01 • 3D Arcade Slide

Hamster Slide

A 3D arcade downhill runner built with Three.js. Steer your hamster ball down a giant sky slide, collect sunflower seeds, hit turbo boost pads, and jump over obstacles to cross the finish line.

⛶ Fullscreen

🐹 Hamster Slide (3D)

Use Left/Right arrows or drag to steer, and Spacebar or tap to jump.

Game 02 • 2D Classroom Slingshot

Paperball

Set inside a 2D classroom, targets descend from the top of the screen while you pull and release crumpled paper balls like a slingshot. You have 3 hearts in the top-left corner—missing a target costs 1 heart.

⛶ Fullscreen

📄 Paperball (2D)

Pull back the paper ball with your mouse or finger and release to hit the falling targets!

Game 03 • 3D Top-View Balance

Bongo

A top-view 3D survival game on a rotating platform. Keep your sphere balanced using the on-screen joystick while colored balls roll across the platform and try to knock you off the edge against the timer.

⛶ Fullscreen

🔵 Bongo (3D)

Use the bottom-left virtual joystick (or arrow keys) to stay on the spinning platform!

Game 04 • 2D Mobile Catcher

Arigato

A vertical mobile 2D arcade game set against a bright light-yellow sky. Move the giraffe left and right to catch and eat falling green leaves—missing 3 leaves triggers a Game Over.

⛶ Fullscreen

🦒 Arigato (2D Mobile)

Slide left and right to catch every falling green leaf before you lose all 3 lives!
